`proc://`: jobs/services; `proc://<id>` status/output; write sends stdin (empty = Enter); `proc://<id>/kill` cancels/stops, omit `content`; `proc://<id>/mode`: `persist`|`session`|`detached`; `proc://<id>/streams` lists your embedded-shell job's captured pipes, `proc://<id>/streams/<n>` reads retained text (1 MiB/pipe; gap notices count bytes; closed pipes remain readable until job eviction). User-shell/PTY pipes are not captured. Read live under `| tail`/`| grep`; never rerun to recover output.
