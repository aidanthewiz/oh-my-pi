`proc://`: jobs, agents, and services; `proc://<id>` status/output; write sends stdin to services only (empty = Enter); `proc://<id>/kill` cancels/stops jobs, agents, and services, send empty `content`; `proc://<id>/mode`: `persist`|`session`|`detached` (services only). `proc://<id>/streams` lists captured pipes and redirected files; `proc://<id>/streams/<n>` reads a captured pipe (1 MiB/pipe; gap notices count bytes; closed pipes remain readable until job eviction). User-shell/PTY pipes are not captured. Read live under `| tail`/`| grep`; never rerun to recover output.
Parked agents or missing jobs do not mean work was lost: check `agent://<id>` and `history://<id>`.
